# Business Travel Policy

## Daily allowance

- Travel inside Saudi Arabia: SAR 400 per employee per day
- Travel outside Saudi Arabia: SAR 750 per employee per day
- The allowance covers meals and local transport. Hotels are booked by the travel desk.

## Booking

- Request flights at least 10 days before travel.
- Economy class for flights under 5 hours.

## Approval

- Trips with a total estimated cost above SAR 10,000 need director approval.
- Trips at or below SAR 10,000 need line manager approval only.

## Claims

- Submit receipts through the HR portal within 30 days of returning.
- Claims without receipts are not reimbursed.
